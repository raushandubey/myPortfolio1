PILOT READY • BENGALURU FLAGSHIP VENTURE (PRE-SEED)

LocalKart

Asset-Light 15-Minute Hyperlocal Quick Commerce Grid (15M+ Kiranas)

The Problem We Solve

Centralized quick-commerce operators (Zepto, Blinkit) burn crores on capex-heavy dark stores (₹40L+ per warehouse) and extract 15%–25% commissions, directly threatening the survival of 15M+ neighborhood kirana stores operating on 8%–12% margins. Centralized warehouses fail in Tier 2/3 clusters where order density cannot sustain high capex.

The LocalKart Solution

Solo-architected and shipped a 4-application cross-platform monorepo turning neighborhood kirana stores into 15-minute digital fulfillment hubs with ZERO dark store capex and ZERO inventory risk. Customers get sub-15 min deliveries from stores within 500 meters, while merchants keep 100% of their margins via flat micro-SaaS subscriptions.

Core Engineering & Business Highlights

  • 4 Decoupled Production Apps: Customer, Store Merchant, Rider Dispatch, and Super Admin panel compiled from a single clean Flutter monorepo.
  • Sub-150ms Real-Time State Sync: Reactive Firestore streams and Riverpod StreamProviders synchronize order status across customer, merchant, and rider instantly.
  • Zero-Latency Audio Dispatch Engine: Looping radar siren override using USAGE_ALARM audio focus with a 15-second dynamic countdown modal for delivery partners.
  • Precision Geohash Engine: Base32 Geohash (150m precision) polygon geofencing preventing street address ambiguity in Indian neighborhoods.
  • Zero-SMS Cost Auth: Custom phone+password authentication over Firebase Auth eliminating SMS OTP costs (~₹1.50/order saved).
  • Zero-CAC Countertop Growth Loop: Countertop QR stands allow merchants to onboard their 150+ daily walk-in customers organically.

Tech Stack & Infrastructure

Flutter 3.x Dart 3.9 Riverpod 2 GoRouter 14 Firebase Firestore Cloud Functions FCM Push Audio Engine
